Makes a timeline clip an audio clip: the sound a [TrackAudio](TrackAudio.md) plays for the clip's span. Needs the sequencer plugin.

## Properties

| Field | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `clip` | none | The audio file: `.mp3`, `.wav` or `.ogg`. |
